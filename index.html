Tabitat

Automatic tab groups, and one tab per page.

Tabitat keeps a Chrome tab strip in order without you thinking about it. Tabs are grouped as they open — by domain, or by rules that understand AWS accounts, Google document types and Jira versus Confluence. Open a link to a page you already have open and the new tab stays while the stale one closes. And a numbered switcher reaches any recent tab, or searches every open one, in two keystrokes.

What it does

FeatureDetail
AWS accountsConsole tabs separate by account, each in its own colour, so Production and Staging never share a group
Google documentsDocs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Drawings, Drive, Sites and Apps Script each get their own group
Jira and ConfluenceOne Atlassian site splits in two; two different sites never merge
Duplicate tabsThe new tab stays, the stale one closes — and pinned tabs, tabs playing audio and tabs holding unsaved input are never closed
Tab switcherA numbered list of recent tabs, or type to search every open tab by title, URL or group

Privacy

No data collection. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party code and no server. The extension's content security policy blocks outbound connections at the browser level, and every permission that could be sensitive is optional and off by default. The detail is in the privacy policy.
